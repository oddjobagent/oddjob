# Release-note rubric

Score the artifact (the agent's final paragraph) against EACH criterion.

## Format

- The artifact is a single paragraph (no headings, no bullet lists, no numbered lists).
- Length is between 40 and 120 words.

## Substance

- Names the specific feature being announced.
- States at least one concrete user benefit.
- Avoids superlatives ("revolutionary", "amazing", "the best", "unparalleled").

## Tone

- Reads like changelog copy: factual and direct, not promotional.
